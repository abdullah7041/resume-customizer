## Comparison with Watheq

Watheq's repository guidance in [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) describes bilingual resume optimization, JD alignment, candidate clarifications, evidence-gated strengths, user-applied changes, cover letters, interview questions and PDF generation. This is implementation documentation, not an independent product-quality benchmark. Arabic extraction/export compatibility must be qualified separately; marketing “ATS-friendly” claims from competitors do not prove their readers perform better.

**Inference:** Arabic/English, ATS scoring, tailoring, templates and “we do not invent facts” cannot alone justify premium pricing: several regional competitors already advertise them. Watheq's more specific opportunity is demonstrating evidence traceability, useful gap handling and reliable per-job exports with a measured before/after workflow. This is a testable positioning hypothesis, not proven demand or an established moat.

**Pricing implication:** The regional public menu includes roughly SAR19–20 one-CV purchases, SAR25–29/month repeat-use tools, SAR35/24-hour application access and SAR99/90-day search access; Vocatra also advertises annual plans starting SAR39. These are different entitlements, not an averaged market price. For a new paid offer, a simple one-time pack tied to completed job applications is easier to compare than unexplained AI credits. A price materially above the subscription anchors needs demonstrated incremental value or more generous usable entitlements. Competitor pages alone cannot establish willingness to pay, sustainable unit economics or the right exact price for Watheq.

## Important uncertainty and exclusions

- Search snippets were inconsistent with opened pages: osaCV's snippet described a free product, while its current opened pricing page states SAR19/CV. The report uses the opened page and flags drift.
- hrai's monthly/yearly subscription extraction is ambiguous; the one-CV price is clear. Do not infer a monthly amount by dividing a crossed-out total.
- Resume Arab is a Gulf-focused adjacent option with public INR49 Basic / INR149 Premium one-time listings, but its pricing page also says Gulf-local builders pay USD. It is excluded from the comparable Saudi-price table because a Gulf checkout total was not established. [Official pricing](https://resumearab.ai/pricing).
- [CV Maker Saudi](https://cvmaker.sa/) publishes Arabic/English tailoring and PDF/Word features, but no readable public price was established; excluded from price comparison.
- No market-share rank, active-customer count, clinical-style ATS success rate, or verified hiring lift was established. Product similarities describe published feature overlap, not implementation equivalence.

## Suggested next validation

Run the same consented Arabic and English source CV plus two real JDs through Watheq and the three closest comparators (Sira, Sera, Ceera). Compare factual additions, omitted evidence, justified gaps, edit effort, exported selectable text and Arabic reading order. Measure completed-application cost using actual limits; do not equate one credit, one AI use, one CV and one job application.
