# Watheq logo direction

## Concept

Watheq needs to feel trusted, Saudi, practical, and premium without becoming a generic AI logo.

The mark combines:

- A resume sheet for the core job-seeker workflow.
- A verification stroke for confidence and truth-checking.
- A subtle Arabic-inspired loop to connect the mark to واثق without depending on text rendering.
- Saudi green with a restrained warm-gold accent, matching the product's Saudi-market positioning.

## Primary asset

Use `public/logo-mark.svg` for the app header and compact brand surfaces.

Use `public/favicon.svg` for browser favicon and compact icon surfaces.

## Recommended usage

- Header icon: replace `/logo-circle.png` with `/logo-mark.svg`.
- Favicon: replace `/favicon-circle.png` with `/favicon.svg`.
- Keep the text lockup in React as `WATHEQ | واثق` instead of baking it into the image. This keeps Arabic/English rendering accessible and consistent with app i18n.

## Colors

- Saudi green: `#006C35`
- Deep green: `#042E24`
- Warm paper: `#FFF9EA`
- Muted gold: `#D6B76A`

## What to avoid

- Sparkles as the brand mark.
- A plain CV icon.
- Too much gold.
- Highly detailed palm/sword motifs that collapse at favicon size.
