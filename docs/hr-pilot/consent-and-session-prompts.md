# Consent and session prompts

Draft operational wording, not a completed consent record or legal assessment. The selected storage/access/deletion operation is in [README](README.md#selected-study-operation). Fill actual owner/contact, verified storage/access, final-session date and deletion date, and the withdrawal contact before using these drafts. Keep filled records privately, outside Git. No invitations or consents have been completed.

## Private invitation drafts

Candidate: “I'm inviting adult volunteer jobseekers to a small Watheq study of a candidate-reviewed evidence worksheet. You would review your supplied resume/job materials and decide exactly what an assigned HR reviewer may see. This is voluntary, is not a hiring decision, and promises no interview. Before you send documents, I'll explain private access, deletion 30 days after the final session, and immediate removal if you withdraw. Would you like the consent details? Please do not send your resume yet.”

HR reviewer: “I'm inviting four active HR reviewers to a small voluntary study comparing supplied resume/job materials with a candidate-approved evidence worksheet. You would review assigned cases in a language and role area you are comfortable with; we would record your requirement judgments, comprehension and active review time. This is not an actual hiring decision. I'll provide access, deletion and withdrawal details before consent. Would you like the study details?”

Send individually only after the owner identifies actual recipients and checks their eligibility; do not use group messages or attach participant materials. Record replies and declines privately. Obtain written affirmative consent covering the specific materials, timing/notes and access rules before collecting documents or assigning cases. No audio/video collection is planned.

## Candidate

“We are testing whether an evidence worksheet helps a recruiter review supplied qualifications. Your resume and statements are not independently verified. This exercise is not a hiring decision, and participation does not promise an interview. We will share only the exact packet you inspect and approve with the assigned reviewer. You may exclude a clarification, decline participation, or withdraw through the agreed contact/process. Here is who can access the materials, where they are stored, and when they will be deleted.”

Record these separately: participation yes/no; exact case and packet version; specific source/clarification IDs permitted to share; exclusions; timing/notes consent; consent date; withdrawal route. Do not treat participation consent as blanket permission for every future packet version or use.

Ask: “Do these quotes belong to this source and role? Does the wording imply anything beyond your supplied facts? Which clarifications may this reviewer see?” Correct the packet before sharing, and preserve the correction count without disclosing excluded content.

## Recruiter

“We are evaluating the review material, not making an actual hiring decision. Use only the supplied resume, job requirements, and assigned materials. Identify supported qualifications and uncertainty; do not assume missing evidence means the candidate lacks the qualification. Your judgments and active review time will be recorded under the agreed access/deletion rules. You may decline or withdraw.”

Confirm language/role familiarity, consent to judgments and timing, and case assignment. Do not record audio/video unless separately agreed.

## Review task

“For each job requirement, record supported, partial, not evidenced, or ambiguous; cite the supplied evidence; state any limitation or clarification you would need.” Start timing when materials open. Log pauses separately. Stop when judgments are submitted. Do not supply the human reference labels or score during the task.

After submission ask:

- What was confusing or difficult to locate?
- Which statement or row would you correct, and why?
- Would you use this material in a similar review? What would have to change?
- If shown an alignment estimate afterward: what does it mean, and what does it not establish?
- What is the difference between a candidate-confirmed statement and independent verification?

Record answers without turning interest into payment intent or recruiting demand. Invite a separate explicit discussion if willingness to pay is a future research question.
