# Watheq Product Marketing Context

Version: 1.0
Updated: 2026-08-11

## Product

Watheq is an Arabic- and English-first AI resume optimizer for Saudi job seekers. A user uploads a resume and pastes a specific vacancy; Watheq scores the evidence-based match, identifies gaps, asks clarification questions where proof is missing, and produces job-specific resume improvements, interview preparation, and ATS-friendly exports.

## Positioning

**For Saudi job seekers actively applying to roles, Watheq is the job-specific resume diagnostic and tailoring workspace that makes a resume more relevant without inventing experience.**

It is not an interview guarantee, an ATS-pass guarantee, or a generic keyword stuffer. The value is a transparent explanation of the gap between a real resume and a real vacancy, followed by reviewable, evidence-grounded changes.

## Primary audience

- Saudi-based Arabic-first and bilingual professionals applying to competitive roles.
- Early-career to mid-career candidates who receive little feedback after applications and are unsure how to tailor a CV credibly.
- Users who want help with Saudi-market conventions, Vision 2030 relevance where it is genuinely present, and bilingual positioning.

## Core pains

- "I do not know why I am not getting interviews."
- "I need a tailored CV for this role, but I do not want AI to make things up."
- "I cannot tell whether ATS keywords matter or how to add them honestly."
- "Paid CV services feel expensive or opaque."

## Proof and trust rules

- Lead with job-specific match evidence and explainable gaps.
- State that suggestions must be reviewed and kept truthful.
- Do not promise interviews, ATS outcomes, hiring outcomes, or unsupported Saudi-market claims.
- Do not use invented testimonials, customer counts, or before/after results.
- Treat 29 SAR/month as planned pricing while the paid plan is not live.

## Current offer and conversion

- Free: resume/job comparison, evidence-grounded suggestions, Arabic/English experience, and four templates.
- Planned Pro: 29 SAR/month; waitlist only until launch terms and entitlements are final.
- Primary CTA: **See your match score — free**.
- Secondary CTA: **Join the pricing waitlist**.

## 30-day objective

Recruit 3–5 consent-based design partners, including at least two Arabic-first users. Success means three guided sessions, two second-vacancy runs, and one credible 29 SAR willingness signal. Never automatically contact prospects.

## Measurement

Track only consent-gated, privacy-safe product signals: guest run start, score bucket, suggestion applied, completion, second-vacancy run, and one-click trust feedback. Do not send resumes, job descriptions, employer names, titles, or local fingerprints to analytics.
